# Cybersecurity Course

## Overview

This course provides essential knowledge in the cybersecurity field
relevant to software testing and testing activities.

## Course Objectives

The course is designed to develop an understanding of fundamental
cybersecurity concepts and their relevance to software testers.

## Course Assessment

The course includes a short knowledge assessment consisting of
10 questions.

To complete the module, a minimum of 6 out of 10 answers must be correct.
